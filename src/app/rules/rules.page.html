Rules Rules

Teams roam London claiming boroughs. Every claim is a challenge someone has written — travel somewhere, find something, hit a target. Most borough points when the clock runs out wins.

The board

{{ activeBoroughs }} boroughs are on offer at any moment, drawn from London's {{ boroughCount }}. Everything on the board is unclaimed — claiming one takes it off the board and into your territory, and another borough takes its place.

A replacement can be any borough that isn't already on the board or held by someone — so anywhere in London is fair game until it has been taken.

The hot borough

One borough on the board is always hot. Claim it and you score {{ hotBonus + 1 }} points instead of 1.

It moves every {{ hotRotation }}, and straight away if someone claims it. A hot borough is always one nobody holds — so it is always up for grabs, and always a decision about whether the journey is worth it.

A borough claimed while hot is locked for the rest of the game, so nobody can take it back off you.

Your hand

Each team holds {{ handSize }} cards, dealt from the challenges everyone has written. Play one and it is spent, whether or not you managed it, and a new card takes its place.

Claim cards are played on the open board. Steal cards are played on boroughs another team already holds.

A steal card stays hidden in your hand — you only ever see "Steal challenge" — until you pick which borough to target. The moment you do, it reveals what it actually is and a countdown starts, {{ stealTimer }} by default. There is no cancelling once it starts; when time's up you still enter whether you managed it, whenever you get to it.

Nothing can check whether you really found that postcard, so you report the outcome yourself. The game runs on trust.

Stealing, and hitting back

A successful steal takes the borough — a two point swing, since they lose one and you gain one.

Fail, and the team you attacked gets {{ counterWindow }} to hit back. Wherever they happen to be, they complete a claim challenge — and if they manage it, they take any borough of yours they like. No travelling to it, no restriction on which one, beyond it not being locked.

The window stays open for the full {{ counterWindow }}, so a failed attempt doesn't end it — they can try again with another card until the time runs out.

Only the team that was attacked gets this, and only against the team that attacked them. Attacking is not free.

Locks

A borough taken by stealing, or claimed while it was hot, is locked for the rest of the game. It cannot be contested again by anyone — it is settled.

So the boroughs still worth fighting over are the ones taken by an ordinary claim. Every steal and every hot claim takes one off the table permanently, and the game tightens as it goes.

Winning

One point per borough held, plus any hot bonuses you banked along the way. Highest score when the clock runs out wins.

The host sets how long the game lasts before starting it, and the clock is visible on the board throughout.
