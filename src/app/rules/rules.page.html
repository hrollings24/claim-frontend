Rules Rules

Teams roam London claiming boroughs. Every claim is a challenge someone has written — travel somewhere, find something, hit a target. Most borough points when the clock runs out wins.

The board

{{ activeBoroughs }} boroughs are on offer at any moment, drawn from London's {{ boroughCount }}. Everything on the board is unclaimed — claiming one takes it off the board and into your territory, and another borough takes its place.

A replacement can be any borough that isn't already on the board or held by someone — so anywhere in London is fair game until it has been taken.

The hot borough

One borough on the board is hot. Claim it and you score {{ hotBonus + 1 }} points instead of 1, draw twice as many power cards, and keep two of them instead of one.

It moves every {{ hotRotation }}, and straight away if someone claims it. A borough can only ever be hot once — once the heat has moved on, it never comes back there.

The bonus point is yours to keep even if the borough is later stolen from you.

Your hand

Each team holds {{ handSize }} claim challenges, dealt from the ones everyone has written. Play one at a borough on the board and it is spent, whether or not you managed it, and a new one takes its place.

Complete it and the borough is yours — and you draw power cards. Each challenge says how many it draws; harder ones draw more.

Nothing can check whether you really found that postcard, so you report the outcome yourself. The game runs on trust.

Power cards

When you draw power cards you keep one and the rest go back in the deck. You can hold up to {{ maxPowerCards }} unplayed at once, and play them whenever you like. Played cards stay on your list, marked as played.

Curses, like No tube, slow another team down. The app only records that you played one — tell them in the group chat, and they stick to it.

Every game draws from one shared deck. The host picks how many of each card go in it from the power card configurations, which anyone can edit. Cards kept are gone from the deck for good, so the numbers set how many can ever be in play.

Stealing

Steal is the most powerful power card. Play it at any borough another team holds — nothing is ever locked — and a steal challenge is revealed, with a countdown, {{ stealTimer }} by default.

Finish it and enter the result before the clock runs out, and the borough is yours — a two point swing. Fail, or let the time run out, and nothing moves — but the team you targeted draws power cards for holding on.

There is no backing out once the challenge is revealed. Pick your target before you see what you'll have to do.

Winning

One point per borough held, plus any hot bonuses you banked along the way. Highest score when the clock runs out wins.

The host sets how long the game lasts before starting it, and the clock is visible on the board throughout.
